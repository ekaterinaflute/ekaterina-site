Ekaterina Kornishina-Braunfeld

Flutist and pedagogue

Biography

Early training

Ekaterina began studying music at the age of four and was a member of the Children’s Choir of the Bolshoi Theatre.

Education

She studied at the Moscow Tchaikovsky Conservatory and later at the Reina Sofía School of Music in Madrid.

Orchestral activity

At the age of eighteen, she joined the National Philharmonic Orchestra of Russia as principal flute.

She has performed as a guest principal flute with orchestras across Europe.

Recordings

In 2020 she released a Rachmaninoff album on the Melodiya label.

Pedagogy

She is the founder of BusyMozart, an international educational project.
